# November 2026 release history

Dates are in Eastern time. The portfolio uses synthetic data to demonstrate the released workflows.

## November 17

The **Simplified** and **Full Fact** Periodical reports gained **Verified Only** and **Verified and Unverified** choices. The export menu groups GIS and advanced downloads. The **Verified Only** ArcGIS event table bundles the CSV with its matching `schema.ini` and uses the same snapshot and event IDs as the Excel report. The menu also opens the approved facility point workflow.

ArcGIS Pro 3.7 imported four synthetic ZIPs from this exact release. The four checks covered both report types with blank measures and documented zeros. Each import joined 18 event rows to Excel, mapped 15 points in WGS 84, and retained three rows without coordinates. The schema preserved calendar text and numeric field types, including wholly blank numeric columns. Documented zeros were preserved and all fields and cells matched the source data. See the [local walkthrough](docs/LOCAL_REVIEW.md#download-a-periodical-report).

## November 16

**Export Periodical Data** added a single-sheet Excel report and an ArcGIS CSV. The reports identify the current review status of shared findings and keep their citations with the event.

## November 13

**Change evidence label** became available from **Facts and evidence** and **Review fact**. Researchers can request a change to an excerpt’s relationship to a finding for independent Supervisor review. After acceptance, they can refresh the evidence in fact review.

## November 11

The incident page brought fact review and the next research steps forward. Saved source excerpts and links became easier to reach, with guidance on the inputs a reviewer needs.
